# Protocol Amendment v2.1 — Relative Teaching Judgments

The first v2 pilot completed 30 isolated subject runs and 10 blinded judge runs. It failed the preregistered measurement gate because 53 of 60 generic 0–2 teaching vectors (88.3%) received the maximum `(2,2,2,2)`, above the 80% ceiling. Baseline checklist scores still had headroom: 7 of 15 averaged Baseline outputs (46.7%) reached 90% of maximum. Aggregate pairwise preferences were Xunxun 5, Baseline 2, tie 8.

The failure is classified as **generic-rubric saturation**, not a failed Xunxun treatment or failed natural-prompt design. No full-suite run is launched from that pilot.

## Change

Remove generic absolute 0–2 ratings. Keep question-specific fact coverage and forbidden-inference checks, then compare A/B directly on four observable teaching outcomes:

1. resolves the novice's expressed confusion;
2. preserves the important conceptual boundary;
3. supplies a rule usable on a new example;
4. achieves the above with lower cognitive cost.

Judges also make one overall A/B/tie choice. Every comparison permits `tie`; fluency alone is not a reason to prefer an answer.

## Revised pilot gate

Use fresh subject and judge samples. Proceed when all hold:

- every pair is isolated and complete;
- fewer than 80% of Baseline outputs receive complete factual coverage with no forbidden inference from both judges;
- at least 20% of all pairwise dimension judgments are non-ties;
- the two judges give the same overall A/B/tie label on at least 60% of replicates.

## Revised full-suite decision

Evidence supports general one-turn value only when all hold:

- among non-tied averaged overall pair decisions, at least 55% favor Xunxun;
- Xunxun's mean factual-coverage delta is nonnegative and no domain drops by more than `0.05`;
- at least two of the four teaching dimensions have positive net Xunxun preference;
- mean final-character ratio is at most `1.5`;
- Treatment trigger rate is at least 80%.

The original v2 pilot files are retained outside the scored v2.1 result set; its aggregate failure is recorded above. This amendment is committed before generating replacement subject outputs.
